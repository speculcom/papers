适配Adaptation

LoRA Learns Less and Forgets LessLoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less

问题：LoRA 省显存是共识，但它相对全参数微调到底少学了多少、又少忘了多少，缺少同条件下的系统对照。
证据位置：摘要在编程与数学两个目标域、约 10 万条指令微调与 20B token 持续预训练两种数据机制下比较；标准低秩设置里 LoRA 明显不如全参数微调，但域外保持更好，且全参数微调学到的扰动秩比典型 LoRA 高 10–100 倍。
作者自陈局限：只覆盖编程与数学两个域、两种数据机制；结论针对标准低秩配置，不覆盖所有 rank/目标模块组合；没有给出所有任务的统一最优配方。
它没主张的：不是 LoRA 一无是处；不是全参数微调一定更好；不是提高 rank 就能完全抹平差距。Problem: LoRA saves memory, but how much less it learns and how much less it forgets than full fine-tuning lacked a controlled comparison.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract compares programming and mathematics, in instruction fine-tuning (about 100K prompt-response pairs) and continued pre-training (20B tokens); in standard low-rank settings LoRA substantially underperforms full fine-tuning, but better preserves out-of-domain performance, and full fine-tuning learns perturbations with 10–100× higher rank than typical LoRA.
What the authors say about limits: only two target domains and two data regimes; conclusions apply to standard low-rank configurations, not every rank or target-module combination; no universal best recipe across all tasks.
What it does not claim: not that LoRA is useless; not that full fine-tuning always wins; not that raising rank closes the gap entirely.
