GACC-Bench: Benchmarking Goal-Adaptive Context Compression Ability across Large Language ModelsGACC-Bench: Benchmarking Goal-Adaptive Context Compression Ability across Large Language Models

问题：长上下文压缩通常按“通用重要性”删，但真实应用先给目标、后给问题；压完才发现关键信息没了。
证据位置：摘要给出 500 个目标、2,571 个问题、281 个文档/智能体轨迹，覆盖 7 个集合；压缩器只看源文、目标和长度预算，评测问题被扣住；10 个压缩器与全上下文仍有明显差距，目标条件化稳定提升、错配目标会掉分。
作者自陈局限：目标由源文问题聚类导出，不等于用户真实目标；固定 reader 隔离了压缩器与阅读器，但也固定了一种阅读行为；281 个上下文、7 个集合仍偏窄；没有端到端延迟/成本报告。
它没主张的：不是通用摘要评测；不是“压缩率越高越好”；不是所有任务都该目标条件化。Problem: long-context compression usually deletes by generic importance, but real applications give the goal first and the questions later; key information is often gone by then.
Where the evidence sits: 500 goals, 2,571 questions and 281 document/agent-trace contexts across seven collections; the compressor sees only source, goal and budget while evaluation questions are withheld; ten compressors still trail full context, and goal conditioning consistently helps while mismatched goals hurt.
What the authors say about limits: goals are derived by clustering source-grounded questions, not real user goals; one fixed reader isolates the compressor but also fixes one reading behaviour; 281 contexts over seven collections is narrow; no end-to-end latency or cost is reported.
What it does not claim: a general summarisation benchmark; that higher compression is always better; that every task should be goal-conditioned.
