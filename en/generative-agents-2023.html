Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior

Problem: how to build believable human-behaviour proxies with LLMs, so multiple agents can socialise and plan in a sandbox.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract describes a memory stream, reflection and dynamic retrieval/planning architecture; a sandbox of 25 agents; the Valentine's party case shows invitations, dates and attendance emerging autonomously; ablation shows observation, planning and reflection each contribute to believability.
What the authors say about limits: a small-town sandbox with 25 agents; believability is author-evaluated, not validated against real humans; memory and reflection cost compute; large-scale social systems are not covered.
What it does not claim: not a social forecasting tool; not a copy of real humans; not a production multi-agent architecture.
