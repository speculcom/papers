MiniMax-01: Scaling Foundation Models with Lightning Attention

Problem: standard attention is too expensive at million-token context, while linear attention often loses capability or engineering efficiency; can lightning attention be combined with MoE and parallel strategies to make long context trainable and servable.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 32 experts, 456B total parameters and 45.9B activated per token; it combines lightning attention with MoE and optimised parallelism plus compute-communication overlap; training reaches 1M tokens and inference extrapolates to 4M; the context window is 20–32× longer than GPT-4o/Claude 3.5 Sonnet, with comparable standard and in-house benchmark results.
What the authors say about limits: the technical report is self-evaluated; 4M is extrapolation, not validated on every task; long-context ability is tied to specific parallelism and kernels; there is no independent reproduction or full safety evaluation.
What it does not claim: not that linear attention fully replaces standard attention; not that 4M context is lossless on every task; not that 456B total parameters are consumer-deployable.
