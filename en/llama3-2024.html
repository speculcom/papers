Scaling laws

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

Problem: can an open-weight 405B dense model approach leading closed models on multilingual, coding, reasoning and tool use, while documenting training and safety evaluation.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a 405B dense Transformer with a 128K context window and says it delivers comparable quality to models such as GPT-4 on many tasks; multimodal experiments add image, video and speech capabilities, but those models are not broadly released.
What the authors say about limits: safety testing cannot be exhaustive, and harmful content remains possible in non-English languages and under adversarial prompts; the multimodal models are still under development and not broadly released; “comparable” comes from their evaluation suite, not an independent head-to-head.
What it does not claim: not a win over GPT-4 on every task; 405B is not a reproducible compute baseline for most teams; the multimodal results are not shipped product capability.
