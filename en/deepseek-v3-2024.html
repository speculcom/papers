Scaling laws

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

Problem: once an MoE language model is scaled up, how do you control training cost, inference cost and load balance instead of only adding parameters.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 671B total parameters with 37B activated per token, MLA and DeepSeekMoE, and pre-training on 14.8T tokens; full training takes 2.788M H800 GPU hours; Section 6 notes that the recommended deployment unit is large and generation speed still has room to improve.
What the authors say about limits: the recommended deployment unit is large enough to burden small teams; generation speed is more than twice DeepSeek-V2 but the authors still see room; the results come from their own training and evaluation, not an independent reproduction.
What it does not claim: not deployable by every team on local hardware; not a blanket win over closed models on every benchmark; 2.788M GPU hours is not a universal cost formula.
