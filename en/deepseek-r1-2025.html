DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning

Problem: complex reasoning has relied on large human-labelled chain-of-thought demonstrations; can reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards alone produce self-reflection, verification and strategy switching.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports pure RL without human-labelled reasoning trajectories; the model beats supervised-demonstration baselines on verifiable tasks in mathematics, coding competitions and STEM; the reasoning patterns from larger models can be used to guide smaller models.
What the authors say about limits: the abstract emphasises verifiable tasks; open-ended writing, subjective judgement and long-horizon planning do not have equivalent evidence; pure RL can bring readability and language-mixing issues and needs a reliable automatic grader; training details and data are not fully open.
What it does not claim: not that pure RL solves every task; not that no supervision is needed anywhere; not that general intelligence has been reached.
