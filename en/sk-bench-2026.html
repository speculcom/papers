sk-bench: A Native-First Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models in Slovak

Problem: multilingual benchmarks often omit Slovak or cover it only through machine translation, which strips morphology in a richly inflected language.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 30 datasets, 33 scored task variants and ten skill categories; eleven resources are newly packaged, including Slovak-adapted IFEval checkers and native grammar/morphology resources; 55 open and closed models are evaluated, and the best open model trails proprietary APIs by 12.6 points.
What the authors say about limits: Slovak only; one harness for 55 models is not every deployment condition; native and translated closed-form data rank models similarly (ρ≥0.98), but translation separates the strongest models less well; human-authored and LLM-generated QA rank differently (ρ=0.72).
What it does not claim: a universal template for all low-resource languages; that continued pretraining always hurts — only that Qwen3-14B lost 13.9 points and instruction tuning restored three quarters; a replacement for native-speaker evaluation.
