Scaling laws

GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol

Problem: how do the two 6.2 tiers divide the work, and what specs and boundaries does the official release state?
Where the evidence sits: the 2026-11-17 release notes say Astra targets advanced reasoning, coding and complex professional work, while Sol combines speed and lower cost for multistep tasks; both improve on their predecessors on demanding tasks while generating fewer output tokens; both accept text and images and return text.
What the authors say about limits: this is a release note, not an independent reproduction; it does not provide latency, long-context ceilings, failure rates or the full system-card data. Read the system card separately; this site does not treat launch claims as measured results.
What it does not claim: a third-party conclusion that it beats every predecessor; that it is faster on every task; an open-weight model.
