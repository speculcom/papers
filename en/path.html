Method lineage: from Transformer to selective state spaces

Ordered by preprint date, showing citation and inheritance only, not rank. Each step links to an entry verified on this site; where the lineage has a gap — a node not separately collected here — it means the original text has not been verified yet, and no second-hand summary is substituted.

  1. 1. 2017: Transformer decouples sequence modelling from recurrence and becomes the shared base for everything after it. entry
  2. 2. 2020.01: Kaplan et al. report power laws for loss against parameters, data and compute, and state limits on theoretical understanding and extrapolation in the appendix. entry
  3. 3. 2020.05: GPT-3 evaluates few-shot prompting, while the authors discuss data contamination and capability limits. entry
  4. 4. 2021.01: Prefix-tuning freezes the base and learns a continuous prefix; it still underperforms full fine-tuning on summarisation. entry
  5. 5. 2021.04: RoFormer injects position information into attention through rotary embeddings, with conclusions bounded by the paper's own experimental setup. entry
  6. 6. 2021.06: LoRA freezes the base and trains low-rank adaptation matrices, reducing trainable parameters and storage overhead; this site has not reproduced it. entry
  7. 7. 2022.01: chain-of-thought prompting writes out intermediate steps, with the authors noting the ability emerges only above roughly 100B parameters. entry
  8. 8. 2022.03: InstructGPT turns following intent into a trainable objective using human preference data, and concedes the models are neither fully aligned nor fully safe. entry
  9. 9. 2022.03: Chinchilla revises the parameter-to-data ratio, finding that both should scale in equal proportion at a fixed budget. entry
  10. 10. 2022.03: self-consistency samples several chains and votes, at the cost of proportionally more computation. entry
  11. 11. 2022.05: FlashAttention reframes the attention bottleneck as memory traffic rather than arithmetic; the result stays exact, but each new implementation needs its own kernel. entry
  12. 12. 2022.10: ReAct interleaves reasoning with acting, gaining traceability while giving up some freedom in reasoning. entry
  13. 13. 2022.10: GPTQ pushes post-training quantisation to 3-4 bits, while its authors note the speed-up comes from less memory movement, not fewer computations. entry
  14. 14. 2022.11: Speculative decoding trades draft-model proposals and parallel target-model verification for inference throughput, with gains depending on acceptance rate. entry
  15. 15. 2022.12: Constitutional AI uses principles for self-critique and AI feedback, while helpfulness labels still come from human supervision. entry
  16. 16. 2023.05: DPO writes preference optimisation as a classification loss, skipping the explicit reward model and sampling, with validation up to 6B parameters. entry
  17. 17. 2023.05: QLoRA combines a quantised base with low-rank adapters; the authors did not establish parity with 16-bit full finetuning at 33B/65B. entry
  18. 18. 2023.05: Tree of Thoughts expands reasoning into a searchable tree, at the cost of more model calls and task-specific evaluation setups. entry
  19. 19. 2023.09: vLLM manages the KV cache with paging and raises serving throughput 2-4 times, while stating the optimisation does not apply to every workload. entry
  20. 20. 2023.12: Mamba removes attention with selective state spaces; the authors leave larger-scale behaviour and downstream ecosystem properties open. entry
