Kimi K2: Open Agentic Intelligence

Problem: open models often trail closed models on agentic tasks; can a very large MoE, a stable optimizer and an agentic data pipeline improve tool use, software engineering and multilingual work.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports a 1T-total, 32B-activated MoE; the MuonClip optimizer enables 15.5T-token pre-training with zero loss spike; post-training includes large-scale agentic data synthesis and joint RL; scores are 66.1 on Tau2-Bench, 76.5 on ACEBench (En), 65.8 on SWE-Bench Verified and 47.3 on SWE-Bench Multilingual, all without extended thinking.
What the authors say about limits: scores focus on non-thinking agentic and software benchmarks; general chat, long-horizon autonomy and safety evaluation are not detailed; 1T total parameters create deployment barriers; training recipe and data are only partly open.
What it does not claim: not a win over closed models on every task; not the same lead under extended thinking; not consumer-hardware deployability because only 32B are active.
