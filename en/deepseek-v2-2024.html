DeepSeek-V2: A Strong, Economical, and Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Language Model

Problem: MoE models carry many parameters and a large KV cache, making inference expensive; can attention and expert routing be made cheaper without giving up capability.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract reports 236B total parameters, 21B activated per token and a 128K context; MLA compresses the KV cache into a latent vector and DeepSeekMoE uses sparse computation to cut training cost; versus DeepSeek 67B it saves 42.5% of training cost, reduces KV cache by 93.3% and raises maximum generation throughput by 5.76×.
What the authors say about limits: this is a technical report with self-evaluation, not an independent reproduction; the 128K context has limited task coverage; MLA/MoE gains depend on specific hardware and parallel implementations, not every stack gets the same numbers.
What it does not claim: not that every MoE must use MLA; not that 21B activated equals a 21B dense model; not that every team sees the same training-cost reduction.
