Multimodal

SenseNova-U1: Unifying Multimodal Understanding and Generation with NEO-unify Architecture

Problem: most vision-language models treat understanding and generation as separate problems, producing fragmented architectures, cascaded pipelines and misaligned representation spaces, which blocks native multimodal capability.
Where the evidence sits: the abstract proposes the NEO-unify native unified paradigm and releases two variants, an 8B dense model and a 30B-A3B mixture-of-experts model; the authors say they rival top-tier understanding-only VLMs on text understanding, vision-language perception, knowledge reasoning, agentic decision-making and spatial intelligence, while keeping semantic and visual consistency on text-to-image, text-rich infographics and interleaved vision-language generation; there is also preliminary evidence for VLA and world-model scenarios.
What the authors say about limits: most results are self-reported without independent reproduction; VLA and world-model evidence is explicitly preliminary; full per-task scores and failure cases are not given; production deployment, licence and inference cost are not reported.
What it does not claim: superiority over every specialist model; a complete world model; an open-source release (not stated in the abstract); a solution to multimodal hallucination.
