多模态Multimodal

Visual Instruction TuningVisual Instruction Tuning

问题：多模态指令跟随数据稀缺，能否用纯语言 GPT-4 生成图文指令数据，再训练端到端多模态模型。
证据位置：摘要称首次用 language-only GPT-4 生成多模态指令数据；LLaVA 连接视觉编码器与 LLM；在合成多模态指令集上相对 GPT-4 得 85.1%；ScienceQA 微调后 92.53%。
作者自陈局限：数据由 GPT-4 生成，继承其偏差；视觉编码器与 LLM 组合固定；评测以合成指令集与 ScienceQA 为主；真实场景鲁棒性未证明。
它没主张的：不是通用多模态助手；不是视觉理解全能；85.1% 不是全面超过 GPT-4。Problem: multimodal instruction-following data is scarce; can language-only GPT-4 generate image-text instruction data to train an end-to-end multimodal model?
Where the evidence sits: the abstract claims the first attempt to use language-only GPT-4 for multimodal instruction data; LLaVA connects a vision encoder and an LLM; it reports 85.1% relative score versus GPT-4 on a synthetic multimodal instruction set and 92.53% on ScienceQA after fine-tuning.
What the authors say about limits: data is GPT-4-generated and inherits its biases; the vision encoder and LLM pairing is fixed; evaluation centres on a synthetic instruction set and ScienceQA; real-world robustness is not established.
What it does not claim: not a general multimodal assistant; not all of visual understanding; 85.1% is not beating GPT-4 across the board.
