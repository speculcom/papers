用超时级联降低 coding agent 推理成本Reducing Inference Cost of Coding Agents with Timeout Cascades

问题：coding agent 很吃 GPU 小时，小模型便宜但怕不稳；能不能先用小模型、失败再升级大模型，在不掉解决率的前提下省钱？
证据位置：论文在 SWE-bench Verified 上评 36 个 2024 年后开放权重模型，发现小模型在 easy/medium 上已接近大模型，差距集中在 hard；用 GLM-4.7-Flash 先跑、超时后升级 GLM-4.7，按 120 分钟超时，easy 任务 GPU 小时从 821 降到 546（-33.5%），全部任务从 2,585 降到 2,160（-16.4%），解决率不掉；简单级联接近 oracle 路由。
作者自陈局限：单次运行、只测一对主模型、只测一个 benchmark；工作负载必须 easy/medium 占多数（SWE-bench Verified 91%），hard 为主时级联会退化；升级是冷重启，不继承小模型的中间状态；墙钟时间反而增加（41.6 分钟/任务 → 65.5 或 84.5 分钟），不适合交互式等结果。
它没主张的：不是所有 coding agent 都能省 33%；不是小模型可以替代大模型；不是级联一定优于学习型路由；不是延迟也会下降。Problem: coding agents burn GPU-hours; small models are cheaper but riskier. Can a small-model-first cascade cut cost without losing resolve rate?
Where the evidence sits: the paper evaluates 36 open-weight models released since 2024 on SWE-bench Verified and finds recent small models close the gap on easy/medium tasks, with the gap concentrated on hard tasks. Using GLM-4.7-Flash first and escalating to GLM-4.7 after a timeout, at a 120-minute timeout, GPU-hours fall from 821 to 546 on easy tasks (-33.5%) and from 2,585 to 2,160 on all tasks (-16.4%) without sacrificing resolve rate; the simple cascade nearly matches an oracle-style router.
What the authors say about limits: single-run evaluations, one primary model pair and one benchmark; the workload must be dominated by easy/medium tasks (91% of SWE-bench Verified), and the cascade degrades when hard tasks dominate; escalation is a cold restart and does not inherit the small model's intermediate state; wall-clock time rises (41.6 min/task to 65.5 or 84.5 min), so it is unsuitable for interactive waiting.
What it does not claim: that every coding agent saves 33%; that small models replace large ones; that a cascade always beats a learned router; that latency also falls.
