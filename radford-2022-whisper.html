规模律Scaling laws

Whisper：用大规模弱监督做稳健语音识别Robust Speech Recognition via Large-Scale Weak Supervision

问题：语音识别通常依赖大量高质量人工转写与领域内微调，换数据集就掉点；能不能用互联网上带噪的转写做弱监督，训练一个不微调也能跨数据集工作的模型？
证据位置：数据与任务设计在 §2，模型与训练在 §3，零样本评测在 §3.x；长音频解码启发式在 §4.5（Table 7）；局限集中在 §6 Limitations and Future Work，结论在 §7。
作者自陈局限：§6 列了五类——解码仍会重复循环、漏掉首尾词、甚至整段幻觉，§4.5 的启发式只是权宜；低资源语言数据少，很多语言不足 1,000 小时，性能差且提升路径是加数据；本文只研究了零样本，没有系统研究微调；Whisper 的稳健性有多少来自编码器、多少来自语言模型解码器仍不清楚；没有用无监督预训练或自训练等辅助目标。
它没主张的：没有说 Whisper 在所有语言上都接近人类——§6 明说很多语言仍很差；没有说零样本一定优于领域内微调——作者反而说高质量监督数据上微调可能更好；也没有说时间戳启发式解决了长音频问题——原文称其为 workaround，仍需更多研究。Problem: speech recognition usually depends on large high-quality human transcripts and in-domain fine-tuning, and degrades when the dataset changes; can noisy internet transcripts provide weak supervision for a model that works across datasets without fine-tuning?
Where the evidence sits: data and task design in Section 2, model and training in Section 3, zero-shot evaluation in 3.x; long-form decoding heuristics in 4.5 (Table 7); limitations in Section 6 and conclusion in Section 7.
Stated limits: Section 6 lists five — decoding can loop, drop the first or last words, or hallucinate an entire transcript, with the 4.5 heuristics merely a workaround; low-resource languages have little data, many under 1,000 hours, and perform poorly; only zero-shot transfer was studied, not fine-tuning; how much robustness comes from the encoder versus the language-model decoder is unclear; and no unsupervised pretraining or auxiliary objectives were used.
What it does not claim: it does not claim Whisper approaches human accuracy in all languages — Section 6 says many remain poor; it does not claim zero-shot always beats in-domain fine-tuning, suggesting the opposite where high-quality supervised data exists; and it does not claim the timestamp heuristics solve long-form audio, calling them a workaround that needs more research.
