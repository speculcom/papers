BERT：面向语言理解的深度双向 Transformer 预训练BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding

问题：此前的语言模型要么是单向的（GPT 式从左到右），要么是两个单向模型拼接（ELMo 式），能不能在一个 Transformer 里同时看左右上下文做预训练？
证据位置：预训练目标在 §3.1（MLM 与 NSP），微调在 §3.2；主结果在 §4 与 Table 1–4；消融在 §5，其中 §5.1 的 Table 5 比较「No NSP」与「LTR & No NSP」，§5.2 的 Table 8 比较不同掩码比例；结论在 §6。
作者自陈：论文没有独立的 Limitations 章节，也没有系统列出局限。§5.1 的消融显示去掉 NSP 会显著伤 QNLI、MNLI 与 SQuAD 1.1；把模型改成从左到右会全面变差，MRPC 与 SQuAD 掉得最多。§5.2 说明微调对掩码策略相当稳健，但纯 [MASK] 策略在特征式 NER 上会出问题。
它没主张的：没有说双向一定优于所有双向变体（只比较了少数消融）；没有说 NSP 本身不可替代（只是去掉会掉分）；也没有给出多语言或长文档的通用结论——实验集中在英文 GLUE、MultiNLI 与 SQuAD。Problem: earlier language models were either unidirectional (GPT-style left-to-right) or a concatenation of two unidirectional models (ELMo-style); can one Transformer pretrain on both left and right context at once?
Where the evidence sits: pretraining objectives in 3.1 (MLM and NSP), fine-tuning in 3.2; main results in Section 4 and Tables 1-4; ablations in Section 5, with Table 5 in 5.1 comparing “No NSP” and “LTR & No NSP”, and Table 8 in 5.2 comparing masking rates; conclusion in Section 6.
What the authors say: there is no separate Limitations section and no systematic list. The 5.1 ablation shows removing NSP significantly hurts QNLI, MNLI and SQuAD 1.1, and switching to left-to-right degrades every task, with the largest drops on MRPC and SQuAD. Section 5.2 finds fine-tuning robust to masking strategies, while a pure [MASK] strategy is problematic for feature-based NER.
What it does not claim: it does not claim bidirectionality beats every bidirectional variant (only a few ablations were run), does not claim NSP is irreplaceable (only that removing it loses points), and gives no general multilingual or long-document conclusion — experiments centre on English GLUE, MultiNLI and SQuAD.
