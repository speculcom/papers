规模律Scaling laws

Kimi K3.1 Pro：智能前沿，更进一步Kimi K3.1 Pro: A Step Further at the Intelligence Frontier

问题：K3.1 Pro 的规模、上下文与产品分工是什么，权重和许可到底开放到什么程度。
证据位置：官方发布页写明 K3.1 Pro 是 K3.1 系列首个模型、3 万亿级参数、100 万上下文；同步推出 Highspeed 高速版，输出速度约 2–3 倍。Hugging Face 官方模型卡进一步给出 2.8T 总参、104B 激活、1,048,576 token 上下文，并写明「open-weight」与自定义 Kimi K3.1 Pro License。发布页承诺的「11 月 4 日前发布完整权重」已兑现，权重文件已上架。
作者自陈局限：发布页没有给训练数据、算力与完整评测口径；模型卡里的评测为厂商自报，部分为内部 benchmark；许可为自定义条款，商用前需读原文。
它没主张的：不是独立第三方评测第一；不是所有任务都适合 2.8T 规模；开放权重不等于可商用无限制。Problem: what are K3.1 Pro's scale, context and product split, and how open are the weights and licence?
Where the evidence sits: the official announcement says K3.1 Pro is the first model in the K3.1 line, with 3T-class parameters and 1M context; a Highspeed variant launches with roughly 2–3x output speed. The official Hugging Face model card gives 2.8T total parameters, 104B active and a 1,048,576-token context, and states that it is an open-weight model under the custom Kimi K3.1 Pro License. The launch-page promise of full weights by November 4 was kept; the files are published.
What the authors say about limits: the page does not disclose training data, compute or full evaluation protocols; model-card benchmarks are vendor-reported and some are internal; the licence is custom, so read the text before commercial use.
What it does not claim: a first place in independent third-party evaluations; that every task benefits from 2.8T scale; that open weights mean unrestricted commercial use.
