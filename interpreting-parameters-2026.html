可解释性Interpretability

Interpreting Language Model ParametersInterpreting Language Model Parameters

问题：机制可解释性在中间表示上找到不少结构，但对“参数与非线性如何在这些表示上计算”进展很少。
证据位置：摘要提出 adVersarial Parameter Decomposition（VPD），把语言模型参数分解为各自只实现算法一小部分的子组件，同时要求少量子组件就能解释网络在任一输入上的行为；优化目标是在大量子组件被消融（包括对抗性选择消融）时仍保持输入输出行为；用子组件间交互序列做一种新的“电路”分析。
作者自陈局限：124 页、47 图，工程量大；方法仍是研究工具，未证明可扩展到前沿规模；子组件的“简单/忠实”由优化目标定义，解释性仍依赖人工判读；对抗消融的评估成本高；没有下游编辑任务的系统验证。
它没主张的：不是完整电路发现；不是可直接用于生产的模型编辑；不是所有参数都能被干净分解。Problem: mechanistic interpretability has found structure in intermediate representations but little progress on how parameters and nonlinearities compute over them.
Where the evidence sits: adVersarial Parameter Decomposition (VPD) decomposes parameters into subcomponents that each implement a small part of the learned algorithm, while requiring only a small fraction to account for behaviour on any input; the objective preserves input-output behaviour under many ablations, including adversarially selected ones; sequences of subcomponent interactions enable a new kind of circuit analysis.
What the authors say about limits: 124 pages and 47 figures make it a heavy research tool; not shown to scale to frontier models; “simple and faithful” is defined by the objective and still needs human reading; adversarial ablation is expensive to evaluate; no systematic validation on downstream editing tasks.
What it does not claim: complete circuit discovery; a production model-editing method; that every parameter decomposes cleanly.
