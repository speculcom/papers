ViT：一张图等于 16×16 个词An Image is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale

问题：Transformer 在 NLP 已经成为标准，但在视觉里通常还要和 CNN 绑定；能不能把图像切成 patch、当成词序列，直接用纯 Transformer 做分类？
证据位置：方法在 §3（patch 切分、线性投影、位置编码）；实验在 §4，包含不同预训练数据规模（ImageNet、ImageNet-21k、JFT-300M）与 ResNet/hybrid 对比；结论在 §5。
作者自陈局限：§5 明确列出三项——还没把 ViT 用到检测与分割；自监督预训练有提升，但与大规模监督预训练仍有很大差距；继续扩大规模可能还会更好，但未验证。§4 也承认在小数据集上 ViT 比同算力 ResNet 更容易过拟合，卷积的归纳偏置在数据少时仍然有用。
它没主张的：没有说 ViT 在所有视觉任务上取代 CNN——结论把检测/分割列为未来工作；没有说自监督已经追平监督；也没有说 patch 16×16 是最优切法（做了 patch size 与位置编码消融，但结论是「规模优先于归纳偏置」这一范围内）。Problem: Transformers were already standard in NLP but usually tied to CNNs in vision; can an image be split into patches, treated as a token sequence, and classified by a pure Transformer?
Where the evidence sits: method in Section 3 (patch extraction, linear projection, positional encoding); experiments in Section 4 across pretraining data scales (ImageNet, ImageNet-21k, JFT-300M) and against ResNets and hybrids; conclusion in Section 5.
Stated limits: Section 5 lists three — ViT has not yet been applied to detection and segmentation; self-supervised pretraining helps but still lags large-scale supervised pretraining by a large gap; further scaling would likely help but was not verified. Section 4 also concedes that on small datasets ViT overfits more than a ResNet of comparable compute, so convolutional inductive bias still matters when data is scarce.
What it does not claim: it does not claim ViT replaces CNNs across all vision tasks — detection and segmentation are left to future work; it does not claim self-supervision has caught up with supervision; and it does not claim 16x16 is the optimal patch size beyond the ablations it ran.
